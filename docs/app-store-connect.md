## Age rating

Answer **Infrequent/Mild** for *Profanity or Crude Humor* and declare the app as **unrestricted web access: No**, then check what Apple computes. Target **12+ or 17+ — never 4+.**

Reasoning: the app hosts open-ended AI conversation about workplace conflict, discipline and terminations. Under-rating an app with unbounded AI output is a known takedown trigger, and the downside of a higher rating here is nearly zero — nobody discovers a frontline management app by browsing 4+.

If Apple's questionnaire asks about user-generated content or chat: the roleplay counterpart is AI, not another human, and there is no user-to-user communication. Say so.

## Privacy — App Privacy answers

Your policy has the disclosures in §2, §4, §6 and §7 of `src/legalContent.js`. Mapped to Apple's categories:

### Data collected and linked to the user

| Category | Type | Purpose | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact Info | Email Address | App Functionality | Supabase auth. Email or Google account, nothing else. |
| User Content | Other User Content | App Functionality | The situations you type, practice transcripts, and per-employee history (`src/lib/employeeMemory.js`). |
| Identifiers | User ID | App Functionality | Supabase user id. |
| Usage Data | Product Interaction | App Functionality | Daily credit metering (`usage_daily`). Metering, not analytics. |
| Purchases | Purchase History | App Functionality | `stripe_customer_id` / `stripe_subscription_id` on `profiles`. |

### Data collected and NOT linked to the user

| Category | Type | Purpose | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnostics | Crash Data | App Functionality | Sentry. `sendDefaultPii: false`, tracing and Session Replay both off — stack traces only, no coaching content. |

### Tracking

**No.** You do no cross-app or cross-site tracking, run no ad network, and use no third-party analytics SDK.

### Two answers to get right

**Audio Data — do NOT declare it.** Dictation uses Apple's own `SFSpeechRecognizer` through the system framework. Your app never receives, stores or transmits the audio; it receives text. The resulting text is already covered by User Content.

**Third-party AI processing.** Coaching text goes to Anthropic and read-aloud text goes to OpenAI. Apple's labels cover data collected *by or on behalf of* your app, so this is disclosed as User Content above — that's the right box. It also has to appear in your privacy policy, which it does (§4). Your in-app consent gate names both providers, which is the 5.1.2(i) requirement and stronger than most apps manage.

## Description (draft)

```
You got promoted for being good at the work. Nobody taught you the
conversations.

Frontline Coach is built for supervisors and managers who run a shift —
restaurants, retail, warehouses, car washes, healthcare, field service. It
turns a messy situation into a plan you can actually say out loud.

COACH ME THROUGH A SITUATION
Describe what happened in your own words. Get back what you own, what the
standard is, what to say, what to ask, and how to follow up.

PRACTICE THE CONVERSATION
Rehearse against an AI employee who pushes back like a real one. Speak your
lines out loud instead of typing them. Then get scored on what actually
landed and what didn't.

HANDLE PUSHBACK
"That's not my job." "Nobody else has to." Know your answer before you're
standing there.

SKILL OR WILL
The same performance problem has different causes. Find out which one you're
looking at before you pick a response.

FOLLOW THROUGH
Track what you said you'd check. Follow-through is the whole job.

Built by a Navy veteran and eighteen-year frontline operator who learned all
of this the wrong way first.

Not legal or HR advice. Always follow your company's policies.
```

Keep the last line. It appears in the app and repeating it here matches what a reviewer sees, which reads as consistency rather than hedging.
